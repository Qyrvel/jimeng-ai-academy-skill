# Community Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful to learners and contributors from different backgrounds and regions.

Be respectful, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior, and do not publish another person's private information. When discussing product behavior, distinguish reproducible observations from assumptions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that are abusive, unsafe, spammy, intentionally misleading, or unrelated to the project. Repeated serious violations may result in loss of participation privileges.

For sensitive concerns, use a private maintainer contact method rather than a public issue when one is available.
